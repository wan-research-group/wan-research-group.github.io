Computing for AI, and AI for computing.

We are the EMBARC Lab (Embodied-AI Architecture & Co-design) at Columbia University. We build cross-layer computing systems, spanning software systems, hardware architectures, and silicon, for emerging AI workloads including embodied, physical, neuro-symbolic, and agentic AI. In the other direction, we develop agentic AI methods that design, optimize, and verify computing systems themselves.

Led by Prof. Zishen Wan · Department of Computer Science · Columbia University
