Computing systems for physical intelligence, co-designed from silicon to systems to agents.

We are the EMBARC Lab (Embodied-AI Architecture & Co-design) at Columbia University. We build the computing substrates that let intelligent machines sense, learn, reason, plan, and act efficiently and reliably in the physical world, and we explore how AI agents can, in turn, design the next generation of computing systems.

Led by Prof. Zishen Wan · Department of Computer Science · Columbia University
