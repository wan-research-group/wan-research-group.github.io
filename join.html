Join Us

Build computing systems for AI, and AI for computing systems

The Wan Lab is a new group at Columbia, which means joining now puts you at the ground floor: close mentorship, first-author opportunities from day one, and a real say in the lab's direction. We work across computer architecture, computer systems, and chip/VLSI design, in two complementary directions: computing for AI, and AI for computing. The lab collaborates closely with industry (IBM, TSMC, Intel, Google, NVIDIA, Qualcomm, Samsung) and offers hands-on hardware prototyping opportunities across FPGAs, GPUs, Jetson platforms, CPUs, and NPUs.

What we look for

There is no single mold. Great fits for the lab usually have strength in one of these, plus curiosity about the rest:

  • Computer architecture: accelerators, memory systems, heterogeneous platforms, software-hardware co-design.
  • Systems: ML and agentic serving, workload characterization, runtime systems, reliability, benchmarking.
  • Chips / VLSI: SoC and RTL design, FPGA prototyping, compute-in-memory, memory-centric computing.
  • Physical & embodied AI: physical AI systems, robotics systems, embedded systems, neuro-symbolic AI.
  • AI for computing: LLM agents for design space exploration, hardware generation, evaluation, and verification.

Strong programming skills and intellectual honesty matter more than a perfect CV. You do not need prior tapeout or robotics experience: depth in one relevant area, plus willingness to learn across layers, is enough. We especially value students who like to build things end-to-end and measure them honestly.

Open positions

PhD Students

Multiple fully funded PhD positions for Fall 2027 (applications due December 2026). You will define and own research agendas across our four thrusts, publish at top venues (ISCA, MICRO, ASPLOS, HPCA, DAC, ISSCC, MLSys, and beyond), with hands-on hardware prototyping and industry internship opportunities.

How: Apply to the Columbia Computer Science PhD program and mention Prof. Zishen Wan in your application. Feel free to also email us (see below).

Postdoctoral Researchers

Openings for postdocs with a strong record in computer architecture, computer systems, chip/VLSI design, or agentic AI for computing. Start date flexible; you will help shape a brand-new lab.

How: Email your CV, a short research statement, and two representative papers.

Master's & Undergraduate Researchers

Columbia MS/undergraduate students can join for research projects, independent study, or thesis work. Strong projects regularly turn into publications and PhD recommendations. A great way to get to know the lab is COMS E6998: AI-Native Computing (Fall 2026).

How: Email your CV, transcript, and a short note on which research thrust excites you and why.

Visiting Scholars & Interns

We welcome motivated visiting students, scholars, and remote collaborators. Tell us what you want to build and how it connects to the lab's research.

How: Email your CV, a one-paragraph project idea, and a short note on which research thrust excites you and why.
